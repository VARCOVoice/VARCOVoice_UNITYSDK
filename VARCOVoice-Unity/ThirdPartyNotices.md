## DSP Algorithms (Audio Effects)

### Freeverb (Reverb)
- **Use**: Implementation of the Schroeder/Moorer reverb algorithm in `FDNReverb.cs`.
- **License**: Public Domain / MIT Compatible
- **Originator**: Jezar at Dreampoint

> The reverb implementation is derivative of the Freeverb algorithm, released into the public domain by Jezar at Dreampoint.

### Audio EQ Cookbook (EQ Filters)
- **Use**: Biquad filter coefficients in `EQLogic.cs` and `BurstDSP.cs`.
- **License**: Public Domain
- **Author**: Robert Bristow-Johnson (RBJ)

> The EQ filter coefficient calculations are based on the "Audio EQ Cookbook" by Robert Bristow-Johnson.

### WSOLA (Pitch Shift)
- **Use**: Time-scale modification algorithm in `WSOLAPitchShift.cs`.
- **License**: MIT Compatible (Standard Algorithm Implementation)
- **Reference**: Verhelst, W. and Roelands, M. (1993). "An Overlap-Add Technique Based on Waveform Similarity (WSOLA) for High Quality Time-Scale Modification of Speech".

### Phase Vocoder (Pitch Shift - Lite)
- **Use**: Frequency domain pitch shifting in `PhaseVocoderPitchShift.cs`.
- **License**: MIT Compatible (Standard Algorithm Implementation)
